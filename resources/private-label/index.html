About artwork, plainly

We handle printing, filling and export documentation, and we can help with label design on request. We are not a design agency and we do not run a full design-and-branding service. Artwork requirements — print specifications, mandatory information for your destination market, file formats — are confirmed per project during quotation.

How a private-label order runs

The same six steps as any other order on How We Work. Private label does not get a separate process; it adds the artwork approval inside step 3.

  1. 1

    Enquiry — tell us your target market, volume, and which products and packaging you have in mind.

  2. 2

    Sample — a sample pack of the varieties you are considering, so you choose style and caliber against the product rather than a datasheet.

  3. 3

    Offer, with artwork — a pro-forma invoice with the agreed specification and pricing, alongside the label and artwork requirements for your market. This is the step private label adds.

  4. 4

    Contract — sales contract, terms and payment, with the approved artwork attached to the specification.

  5. 5

    Production — processing, grading, filling, labelling to the approved artwork, and quality checking at the approved partner facility.

  6. 6

    Shipment — export documentation pack and shipment, prepared and coordinated by Triple Company for Industrial Development.
