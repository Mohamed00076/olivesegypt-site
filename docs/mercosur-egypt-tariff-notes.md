## The agreement

- Signed 2 August 2010 (San Juan). In force for Mercosur on
  **1 September 2017** (Brazil: Decree 9.229 of 6 December 2017, recitals;
  Siscomex page).
- Tariff elimination categories, Chapter I, **Article 11(1)**:
  - A: on entry into force;
  - **B: in 4 equal stages, the first on entry into force and the other three
    at 12-month intervals** (so the last stage fell on 1 September 2020);
  - C: 8 equal stages; D: 10 equal stages (the last on 1 September 2026);
  - E: as the Joint Committee decides.
- The preference needs the origin requirements, including the certificate of
  origin, Chapter I **Article 14** and Chapter II (Art. 19(1)). Specific origin
  rules: Annex II.4. Certificate model: Annex II.1. Siscomex: the certificate
  and the request form are completed in English.
- The agreement covers the import duty (II) only. IPI, PIS/COFINS-Importação,
  ICMS and fees still apply.
