## Questions for counsel (LGPD)

1. Does the LGPD apply? The contacts are businesses, but their staff are natural persons in Brazil, and the page is aimed at Brazil (LGPD art. 3).
2. Which legal basis: pre-contract steps at the data subject's request, or legitimate interest?
3. Is an *encarregado* (data protection officer) required, or does the ANPD's small-agent regime apply?
4. International transfer: the data is held by Neon and Netlify outside Brazil. Which transfer mechanism applies?
5. How should the data-subject rights (art. 18) and the retention periods be worded? Several retention items on the English page are still open.
6. Does the cookie banner (reject, settings and accept at the same level; analytics off by default) meet ANPD guidance as it is?
7. Is the Portuguese policy the binding version for Brazilian visitors, or does the English one prevail?
