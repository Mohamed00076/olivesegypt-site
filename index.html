Our Story

From Egyptian groves to dining tables around the world

Triple Company was founded with a single conviction: Egyptian table olives — especially the Aggizi variety native to the Nile Delta — are among the finest in the Mediterranean world, yet largely unknown to global buyers.

We work with a partner processing facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone to close the gap between artisan quality and industrial reliability. Every jar, tin, and barrel we ship is held to the same standard — traceable and quality-checked.

    About Triple Company for Industrial Development

    Triple Company for Industrial Development is an Egyptian company preparing to export premium table olives, founded in 2024 and built on 15+ years of its founders' experience in the olive trade. We supply bulk and private-label Aggizi, Toffahi, Manzanilla, Hamed, and Natural Black table olives, plus stuffed and flavored varieties (green olives stuffed with pepper, garlic, or almond) and complementary antipasti (marinated artichoke hearts, pepperoncini, jalapeño). Our olives are sourced from a partner processing facility that is Kosher, Halal, ISO 22000, and NFSA certified. Head office in 5th Settlement, Cairo; partner processing facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone, Egypt.
