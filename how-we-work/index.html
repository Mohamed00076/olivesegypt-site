How We Work

A Business Model Built for Serious Buyers

We work with a partner processing facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone — not a broker — and we're preparing to deliver Egyptian table olives, sourced through that certified partner facility, to importers, distributors, and retail chains worldwide.

Our Model

Why Importers Choose to Work With Us

Partner-Facility Sourcing

We work with a trusted partner processing facility — no unnecessary intermediaries — so you get consistent quality.

Flexible Commercial Terms

T/T deposits, L/C at sight, and negotiable terms for established partners. All transactions in USD or EUR, with pro-forma invoices issued fast.

Private Label & OEM

Your brand, your label, your language. Full design-to-shelf private label service across glass, tin, bucket, and barrel formats.

End-to-End Logistics

Complete export documentation, certificate of origin, health certificate, and port handling — arranged for each container to support smooth customs clearance.
