Triple Company for Industrial Development — Company Overview

Egyptian Table Olive Export · Company Overview · Revised 2026-09-06

Who we are

Triple Company for Industrial Development is a newly established Egyptian table-olive export company, based at Ouroba Square (ميدان العروبة), 5th Settlement, New Cairo, Cairo, Egypt. We are actively developing our first international buyer relationships. We are direct about this because we would rather earn your trust with an honest first order than with an inflated track record.

How we operate

We do not own the processing facility. We work with an approved partner arrangement for processing and storage: Triple Company for Industrial Development handles sourcing, quality specification, sales and export logistics, while the physical processing and brining takes place at a partner facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone.

  • We handle — sourcing and lot selection, product specification, buyer communication and commercial terms, export coordination and port handling, and the export documentation pack.
  • The approved partner facility handles — processing and brining, grading to the agreed caliber, filling and packing, labelling to the approved artwork, and holding whatever certifications it holds, in its own name.

The range

Eleven products across three groups.

  • Green olives — Aggizi, Toffahi, Hamed, Manzanilla, and Pepper Stuffed Green Olives.
  • Black olives — Kalamata, Natural Black, and Oxidized Black.
  • Specialty and antipasti — Sliced Jalapeño Peppers, Marinated Artichoke Hearts, and Pepperoncini Peppers.

Calibers, brine specifications and formats for each product are published on the product catalogue and on each product’s own page.

Packaging

Glass jars, tin cans, plastic buckets, wooden barrels (for bulk brine), and vacuum pouches, depending on the product and order volume — these are the packaging formats we have actually confirmed as available. Private-label and OEM packaging is available across glass, tin, bucket and barrel formats; artwork, labelling and minimum quantities for custom packaging are confirmed per project.

Commercial terms

  • Minimum order — one 20ft container, approximately 16–18 metric tons. Smaller trial quantities may be possible depending on variety and format. Exact MOQ is confirmed during quotation.
  • Payment — typically 30% T/T deposit against 70% on bill-of-lading copy.
  • Incoterms and lead time — confirmed per order.
  • Documentation — commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary or health certificate, and bill of lading, prepared for every shipment.

Markets we are engaging

We are actively engaging buyers in Africa, the Middle East and Asia. These are the markets we are building relationships in — not markets we currently serve with an established shipping history. Triple Company for Industrial Development has not yet completed an export shipment.

Where our documentation stands

Quality and documentation information is set out in three tiers on our Quality & Documentation page: what is published on the site today, what we confirm in writing for your specific order, and what is still being established. No certificate name, number, issuing body or scope appears anywhere on our site until it has been checked against the actual certificate document.
