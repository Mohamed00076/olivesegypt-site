Products available for private label

Ten of our eleven products: Aggizi, Toffahi, Hamed and Manzanilla green olives; Pepper Stuffed Green Olives; Natural Black and Oxidized Black olives; and Sliced Jalapeño Peppers, Marinated Artichoke Hearts and Pepperoncini Peppers.

Packaging

Our private-label offer covers four formats: glass jars, tin cans, plastic buckets and barrels. Vacuum pouches are an available packaging format as well; whether they suit a private-label programme is confirmed during quotation. Exact sizes, case configurations and minimum quantities per format depend on the product and the order volume.

About artwork, plainly

We handle printing, filling and export documentation, and we can help with label design on request. We are not a design agency and we do not run a full design-and-branding service. Artwork requirements — print specifications, mandatory information for your destination market, file formats — are confirmed per project during quotation.

How a private-label order runs

The same six steps as any other order. Private label does not get a separate process; it adds the artwork approval inside step 3.

  • 1. Enquiry — your target market, volume, and the products and packaging you have in mind.
  • 2. Sample — a sample pack of the varieties you are considering.
  • 3. Offer, with artwork — a pro-forma invoice with the agreed specification and pricing, alongside the label and artwork requirements for your market.
  • 4. Contract — sales contract, terms and payment, with the approved artwork attached to the specification.
  • 5. Production — processing, grading, filling, labelling to the approved artwork, and quality checking at the approved partner facility.
  • 6. Shipment — export documentation pack and shipment, prepared and coordinated by Triple Company for Industrial Development.
