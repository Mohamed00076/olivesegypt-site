Product Specifications · Revised 2026-09-01

Aggizi Green Olives

Egypt's most exported olive variety. Firm texture, mild brine, bright green color. Grown in the Nile Delta. Available whole, pitted, or cracked. Calibers 141/160 to 231/260.

Toffahi Green Olives

Premium Egyptian olive from Fayoum. High flesh-to-pit ratio, slightly sweet flavor. Available whole, pitted, or stuffed. Calibers 101/110 to 161/180.

Hamed Green Olives

Large-caliber olives from Egypt's North Coast. Cracked and marinated with herbs. Calibers 161/180 to 261/290. Popular in Middle Eastern import markets.

Manzanilla Green Olives

Internationally recognized Spanish variety grown and processed in Egypt. Available whole, pitted, or stuffed with pimiento, almond, or garlic. Full caliber range 101/110 to 231/260.

Natural Black Olives

Naturally ripened Egyptian black olives. No iron gluconate, no artificial coloring. Available whole, sliced, or pitted in brine or vacuum-sealed. Calibers 141/160 to 261/290.

Stuffed Green Olives

Premium Manzanilla and Aggizi olives stuffed with pimiento, almond, garlic, or lemon under hygienic, quality-controlled conditions at our partner facility. Available in glass jars and tins for European retail and food service.

Oxidized Black Olives

California-style black olives darkened by controlled oxidation for a uniform jet-black color and smooth, mild flavor. Whole, sliced, or pitted. Ideal for pizza toppings, food service, and retail cans. Grown in the Nile Delta, Egypt.

Sliced Jalapeño Peppers

Crisp green jalapeño rings pickled for a bright, medium heat. Available as sliced rings or whole. A high-demand food-service line for nachos, pizza, and Tex-Mex applications.

Marinated Artichoke Hearts

Tender artichoke hearts marinated in oil with Mediterranean herbs. Available as quarters, hearts, or grilled. A premium antipasto line that complements our olive range for delis, retail, and food service.

Pepperoncini Peppers

Mild, tangy golden-green peppers pickled in brine (Golden Greek style). A versatile antipasto and sandwich pepper in strong demand across European and North American retail.
