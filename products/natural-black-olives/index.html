Natural Black Olives

Origin: Nile Delta, Egypt

Product Profile

Naturally ripened on the tree and processed without oxidation agents. Deep purple-black color, soft texture, mild flavor. No iron gluconate, no artificial coloring.

Available Formats
Whole
Calibers (count / kg)
140-360
Packaging OptionsGlass jars, tin cans, plastic buckets, or plastic barrels (brine), subject to product and order volume.

Brine Specification

Salt

4–6%

0.1–0.2% citric

pH

6.0–7.0
