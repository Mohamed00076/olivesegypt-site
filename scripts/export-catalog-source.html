Company Profile & Quality Status

Our Story

Triple Company for Industrial Development is an Egyptian table-olive supplier based in Cairo, Egypt. We are preparing for international export of the approved product range for bulk, wholesale, food-service, retail, and private-label buyer discussions. As a newly established company, we are building our supply model through approved partner arrangements. Product specifications, samples, packaging, documentation, MOQ, pricing, and delivery terms are confirmed according to the product and buyer request. We are actively developing buyer relationships and will confirm the applicable commercial and documentation requirements during quotation.

Certifications & Quality Documentation

Quality and documentation information is being prepared. Current product specifications and available documents can be requested by qualified buyers.

We work through a partner processing arrangement — Triple Company does not own or operate a processing factory. Products are sourced and processed through an approved partner facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone. Any certification claim refers to that partner facility, never to Triple Company itself.

Product Range

Aggizi, Toffahi, Hamed, and Manzanilla Green Olives · Natural and Oxidized Black Olives · Pepper Stuffed Green Olives · Marinated Artichoke Hearts · Pepperoncini Peppers · Sliced Jalapeño Peppers. Full specifications on pages 4–6.

Requesting Current Documentation

Qualified buyers can request the current status of our quality documentation, along with product specification sheets, at any time via olivesegypt.com/contact.

Product Specifications

Green Olives

Aggizi Green Olives

Egypt's most exported olive variety. Firm texture, mild brine, bright green color. Grown in the Nile Delta.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Cracked  ·  Caliber: 141/160 – 231/260

Toffahi Green Olives

Premium Egyptian olive from Fayoum. High flesh-to-pit ratio, slightly sweet flavor.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Stuffed  ·  Caliber: 101/110 – 161/180

Hamed Green Olives

Large-caliber olives from Egypt's North Coast. Cracked and marinated with herbs. Popular in Middle Eastern import markets.

Styles: Cracked, Marinated  ·  Caliber: 161/180 – 261/290

Manzanilla Green Olives

Internationally recognized Spanish variety grown and processed in Egypt.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Stuffed (pimiento, almond, or garlic)  ·  Caliber: 101/110 – 231/260

Brine/pH parameters and per-product packaging sizes: confirmed during quotation — not published for these varieties at this time.

Black Olives & Stuffed Varieties

Natural Black Olives

Naturally ripened Egyptian black olives. No iron gluconate, no artificial coloring.

Styles: Whole, Sliced, Pitted — in brine or vacuum-sealed  ·  Caliber: 141/160 – 261/290

Oxidized Black Olives

California-style black olives darkened by controlled oxidation for a uniform jet-black color and smooth, mild flavor. Ideal for pizza toppings, food service, and retail cans.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Sliced  ·  Caliber: Confirmed during quotation

Pepper Stuffed Green Olives

Premium Manzanilla and Aggizi olives stuffed with pimiento, almond, garlic, or lemon at our partner facility. Available in glass jars and tins for European retail and food service.

Styles: Stuffed (pimiento, almond, garlic, or lemon)  ·  Caliber: Confirmed during quotation  ·  Packaging: Glass jars and tins

Specialty, Peppers & Antipasto

Marinated Artichoke Hearts

Tender artichoke hearts marinated in oil with Mediterranean herbs. A premium antipasto line that complements our olive range for delis, retail, and food service.

Styles: Quarters, Hearts, Grilled  ·  Not a caliber-graded product

Pepperoncini Peppers

Mild, tangy golden-green peppers pickled in brine (Golden Greek style). A versatile antipasto and sandwich pepper in strong demand across European and North American retail.

Styles: Whole, pickled  ·  Not a caliber-graded product

Sliced Jalapeño Peppers

Crisp green jalapeño rings pickled for a bright, medium heat. A high-demand food-service line for nachos, pizza, and Tex-Mex applications.

Styles: Sliced rings, Whole  ·  Not a caliber-graded product
