Company Profile & Quality Status

Our Story

Triple Company for Industrial Development is an Egyptian table-olive supplier based in Cairo, Egypt. We are preparing for international export of the approved product range for bulk, wholesale, food-service, retail, and private-label buyer discussions. As a newly established company, we are building our supply model through approved partner arrangements. Triple Company has invested in a dedicated brining line at our partner processing facility — now operational, as part of our ongoing investment in the facility's capacity (approximately 2,000 metric tons total). Further equipment upgrades are planned at the facility, with Triple Company continuing to invest alongside our partner as the relationship grows. Product specifications, samples, packaging, documentation, MOQ, pricing, and delivery terms are confirmed according to the product and buyer request. We are actively developing buyer relationships and will confirm the applicable commercial and documentation requirements during quotation.

Certifications & Quality Documentation

Quality and documentation information is being prepared. Current product specifications and available documents can be requested by qualified buyers.

We work through a partner processing arrangement — Triple Company does not own or operate a processing factory. Products are sourced and processed through an approved partner facility in the 10th of Ramadan Industrial Zone. Any certification claim refers to that partner facility, never to Triple Company itself.

Product Specifications

Green Olives

Aggizi Green Olives

Egypt's most exported olive variety. Firm texture, mild brine, bright green color. Grown in the Nile Delta.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Cracked  ·  Caliber: 140-360

Toffahi Green Olives

Premium Egyptian olive from Fayoum. High flesh-to-pit ratio, slightly sweet flavor.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Stuffed  ·  Caliber: 140-360

Manzanilla Green Olives

Internationally recognized Spanish variety grown and processed in Egypt.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Stuffed (pepper or carrot)  ·  Caliber: 140-360

Brine/pH parameters and per-product packaging sizes: confirmed during quotation — not published for these varieties at this time.

Black Olives & Stuffed Varieties

Kalamata Olives

Internationally recognized Greek variety, grown and processed in Egypt. Deep purple-black color, smooth meaty texture, distinctive almond shape.

Styles: Whole, Pitted  ·  Caliber: Confirmed during quotation

Natural Black Olives

Naturally ripened Egyptian black olives. No iron gluconate, no artificial coloring.

Styles: Whole, Sliced, Pitted — in brine  ·  Caliber: 140-360

Oxidized Black Olives

California-style black olives darkened by controlled oxidation for a uniform jet-black color and smooth, mild flavor. Ideal for pizza toppings, food service, and retail cans.

Styles: Whole, Pitted, Sliced  ·  Caliber: Confirmed during quotation

Pepper Stuffed Green Olives

Premium Manzanilla and Aggizi olives stuffed with pepper or carrot at our partner facility. Available in glass jars and tins for European retail and food service.

Styles: Stuffed (pepper or carrot)  ·  Caliber: Confirmed during quotation  ·  Packaging: Glass jars and tins

Pickled Vegetables

Marinated Artichoke Hearts

Tender artichoke hearts marinated in oil with Mediterranean herbs. A premium antipasto line that complements our olive range for delis, retail, and food service.

Styles: Quarters, Hearts, Grilled  ·  Not a caliber-graded product

Pepperoncini Peppers

Mild, tangy golden-green peppers pickled in brine (Golden Greek style). A versatile antipasto and sandwich pepper in strong demand across European and North American retail.

Styles: Whole, pickled  ·  Not a caliber-graded product

Sliced Jalapeño Peppers

Crisp jalapeño rings pickled for a bright, medium heat, in green or red. Packed in glass jars from 320ml to 1050ml, in 65mm, A9, A10 and A12 cans, and in a 4kg PET pail. A high-demand food-service line for nachos, pizza, and Tex-Mex applications.

Styles: Sliced Green Rings, Sliced Red Rings, Whole  ·  Not a caliber-graded product
