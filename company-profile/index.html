Our Story

Triple Company for Industrial Development is an Egyptian table-olive supplier based in Cairo, Egypt. We are preparing for international export of the approved product range for bulk, wholesale, food-service, retail, and private-label buyer discussions. As a newly established company, we are building our supply model through approved partner arrangements. Product specifications, samples, packaging, documentation, MOQ, pricing, and delivery terms are confirmed according to the product and buyer request. We are actively developing buyer relationships and will confirm the applicable commercial and documentation requirements during quotation.
